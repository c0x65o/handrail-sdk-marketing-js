## Tracking and business outcomes

Tracking is a product task: “How will this campaign know it worked?” It follows
connection/account choice and feeds launch readiness. Existing conversion IDs and
ownership verification are necessary binding evidence, not proof of website firing.

| Business outcome | Supported local semantics | Tracking types / future guided setup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visits / interest | Consented eligible paid-click receipts; provider impressions/clicks distinct | Destination URL/campaign linkage; existing site analytics/pixel/tag binding and click verification |
| Acquisition inquiries | `form_completed`; completed submissions vs unique people/legacy leads | Trusted completed-form receipt; exact provider lead conversion binding where eligible |
| Qualified acquisition leads | Separate qualification event | Authorized CRM/host qualification receipt; never infer from form or conversation |
| Customers / revenue | Purchases and unique customers distinct; media CAC/ROAS with stated window/currency | Trusted order/payment receipt with stable identity and explicit revenue/refund policy |
| Recruitment | `application_completed`, `applicant_qualified`, `hired`, explicit recruitment purpose | Existing applicant source and exact ApplicantRequestMOU mapping only where required; MOU event is not automatically every application or a hire |

The proposed Tracking screen asks for the outcome, then offers **Bind existing
tracking** or **Set up website tracking**. Show site/domain ownership, provider and
account, tag/pixel/conversion name, purpose and event rule with resolved labels.
Admin implementation instructions are an expandable, copyable installation guide
for the existing host/site analytics integration; do not create a second analytics
store. Installing a tag, creating a conversion or transmitting a provider event is
a separately authorized change. Missing hooks show the responsible site admin and
what is needed; saving a binding alone leaves “Not tested”.

**Test sequence:** issue a short-lived test session bound to project/site/outcome;
open the actual destination; complete the intended consented test interaction;
receive an authenticated collector receipt with occurred/received time, source,
dedupe key and QA flag; correlate it to that test session; show field-level safe
diagnostics. A timeout shows “No test event received” and checks installation,
consent, network delivery, wrong domain/event/account and collector authentication.
Never simulate a green tick from an entered ID or an agent's claim.

Display independent evidence stages:

1. **Website observed:** the designated interaction fired in the real destination;
   browser evidence alone cannot authenticate ingestion.
2. **Locally received:** trusted ingestion accepted/deduplicated the source event;
   store the safe receipt, not contact data in general logs.
3. **Provider accepted:** only if an authorized provider transport supplied its
   acceptance receipt; an outbox entry is “Queued”, not accepted.
4. **Matched:** only actual provider matching diagnostics support this label;
   acceptance does not prove identity match. Missing diagnostics are unavailable.
5. **Attributed:** first-party attribution or provider attribution is calculated
   with its own rule/window; neither proves the other. Test events never count.

QA exclusion must be carried explicitly across collector, imports and reports.
Bind source identity from the authenticated connector; dedupe by source plus
stable event-specific receipt, preserving original payload evidence across retries.
One person can submit several times: report historical submissions separately from
people, keep original attribution and exclusion receipts, and do not re-ingest old
records to silently apply a new rule. New SDK first-party attribution remains
`last-paid-click-7d-v1`, no view-through; ambiguity/missing consent stays unresolved
or unattributed. Coverage attestations are outcome-purpose-specific; no observed
rows without completeness evidence means unknown, not zero.

Consent purpose, revocation and permitted identifiers constrain collection and any
future sharing. No fingerprinting or transcript/contact data in aggregate diagnostics.
Retention/deletion policy must apply to raw events, derived links and reports without
claiming unapproved legal exceptions. This milestone introduces no consent policy.

**LinkedIn Conversions API:** the supplied app `265827087` product-level approval
is not account eligibility, OAuth scope, token authority, conversion ownership,
event transport or matching/attribution proof. Current HostAgent scopes do not
include a qualified Conversions API sending path; the current outbox is local.
Future qualification must identify the exact app/API/version and approved sending
permission from official docs, authorized token/account/organization relationship,
exact conversion rule/association, collector consent/dedupe/QA handling, transport
receipt and failure/recovery behavior. Do not add scopes, mint grants or send events
in this milestone. Never substitute generic advertising permission for that work.

## Campaign and draft lifecycle

This is a later coherent lifecycle tranche; Connections must link to it without
pretending the existing save/edit form completes it. Drafts may be incomplete,
unconnected and safely saved; readiness does not erase those values.

| Action | Proposed visible semantics and safeguards |
| --- | --- |
| Edit | Admin/editor edits an expected revision; validate safe local fields, preserve incomplete draft. Conflicts show latest vs own changes and explicit reapply/copy; no silent last-write wins. New campaign material invalidates dependent eligibility/packets; active provider material is not overwritten by a draft edit |
| Copy | New local draft with a new identity and “Copy of …” label, retaining permissible material/provenance only. No grants, approvals, provider object IDs, spend reservations or operation identities copied; project/account destination changes force revalidation |
| Archive | Reversible hiding from default list, visible Archived filter and reason/time/actor. Local draft archive has no provider effect. For enabled/unknown campaigns block routine archive until observed stopped/reconciled; never imply archive pauses delivery |
| Recover | Authorized admin/editor restores archived local record under revision check. Does not revive revoked grants, expired approval or provider objects; show current readiness dependencies |
| Delete permanently | Separate action-time human approval showing exact record/revision, affected assets/history and retention consequences. Cancel is default. No agent decision; admin with explicit deletion permission. Conflict/changed impact/expiry invalidates approval. No automatic deletion because a record was archived |
| Connected campaign constraints | Paused/enabled/unknown campaigns retain receipts, leases, reservations and historical reporting. Unknown effects must reconcile before destructive lifecycle actions. Provider pause is a separate permitted operation; provider object deletion is not an implied SDK action |

Archive is not a retention purge. Preserve operation identities/dedupe tombstones,
authority receipts and required audit history; release a media blob only when no
retained record references it and approved retention permits it. Explain what can
actually be removed before asking for approval. Retention duration, legal holds,
personal-data erasure and treatment of derived historical aggregates require a
host policy decision before deletion implementation; there is no invented default
number of days. Until configured, permanent delete is unavailable with a reason.
No hidden provider object deletion, cascading account revocation or spend release.
This missing permanent-deletion policy does not block reversible archive/recover
with no purge, or Connections implementation. Scope retention decisions to erasure.

## Studio, launch and results continuity

Studio keeps a brief, audience hypothesis, destination, creative variants and one
creative-set lineage. The user compares actual retained assets and editable copy,
sees rights/moderation status, and deliberately selects a material version.
Processing/failed/unknown generation stays visible with reconcile actions. Paid
image/video requests require their own bounded human authority and billing policy;
an ad packet cannot authorize generation. Editing/cropping creates a version.

Launch review shows actionable dependencies: account/identity, exact format and
audience, destination digest, tracking evidence for the chosen goal, money/time,
current grants and actual capability receipt. Non-conversion traffic must not claim
conversion readiness; a conversion objective cannot proceed with an untested or
unqualified event path. Preparation of paused objects and activation require
separate exact human decisions. Every packet includes provider-native plan,
project/account/grant revision, immutable media bytes, copy, targeting, destination,
currency, budget semantics, schedule/timezone and expiration. “Ready to review”
is not approval. Changed material or authority invalidates it. Host's existing sole
executor dispatches; SDK starts no competing scheduler/controller.

Results shows chosen time window, provider reporting basis, source timestamp,
coverage and currency. Provider enabled intent, review pending, delivering and
ended observations are distinct. Show spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions, completed
submissions, people, qualifications, customers/revenue and recruitment outcomes
with definitions; no empty charts that imply zero. Media CAC excludes untracked
creative/labor costs; ROAS is not profit. Provider conversions are not added to
first-party conversions. Linked conversations require separate sales access;
agent drafting confers no message-send permission. The latest explicit owner
request names CTR, impressions and conversions; implement those with definitions,
windows and separate provider/first-party counts. Historical ambiguous wording is
retained in the original evidence, not carried forward as a new question or blocker.
